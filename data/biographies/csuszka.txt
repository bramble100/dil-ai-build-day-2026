Csuszka — Dummy Biography
===================

Csuszka is a female Hungarian Vizsla born on 14 March 2019 in Budapest, Hungary.
She was bred by the Arany Vizsla kennel and joined her family at the age of 8 weeks.

Physical description
--------------------
Csuszka has the characteristic golden-rust coat of the Hungarian Vizsla breed.
She weighs 22 kilograms and stands 57 centimetres at the shoulder.
Her eyes are a warm amber colour, matching her coat perfectly.
She has a lean, muscular build typical of working Vizslas.

Personality
-----------
Csuszka is an affectionate and energetic dog with an exceptional nose.
She is highly food-motivated, which made her training particularly straightforward.
She bonds strongly with her owner, Veronika, and becomes anxious if left alone for more than two hours.
She is gentle with children and friendly with other dogs, especially her companion Panir.

Key life events
---------------
- April 2019: Joined her family in Budapest as an 8-week-old puppy.
- June 2020: Completed her first hunting aptitude test (vadászati alkalmassági vizsga) with an excellent result.
- September 2021: Participated in her first dog show at the Budapest Kennel Club exhibition, receiving a "Very Good" rating.
- March 2022: Gave birth to a litter of six puppies — four females and two males.
- July 2023: Completed a 5-kilometre open-water swim in Lake Velence, setting a personal distance record.
- November 2023: Achieved her Field Trial Junior Champion title.

Favourite activities
--------------------
Csuszka's favourite activity is retrieving from water. She will enter any body of water regardless of temperature.
She also enjoys long trail runs in the Buda Hills, often covering 10–15 kilometres with her owner.
Her favourite route is the Normafa loop, which she has completed more than 40 times.
She dislikes thunderstorms and fireworks, seeking comfort by pressing against her owner's legs.

Relationship with Panir
-----------------------
Csuszka and Panir have lived together since Panir arrived as a puppy in 2021.
Csuszka initially asserted her seniority but quickly became Panir's closest companion and play partner.
The two dogs sleep side by side and become visibly distressed when separated.

Health
------
Csuszka has been in excellent health throughout her life.
She receives annual vaccinations and has been screened clear for hip dysplasia (HD-A rating).
In February 2022, she underwent a minor surgical procedure to remove a benign skin growth on her left shoulder.
She is fed a raw diet supplemented with fish oil and has maintained a stable weight since adulthood.
