Panir — Dummy Biography
=================

Panir is a male Hungarian Vizsla born on 3 July 2021 in Győr, Hungary.
He was bred by the Duna-parti Vizsla kennel and joined his family at 9 weeks of age.

Physical description
--------------------
Panir has a slightly darker golden-rust coat compared to Csuszka, with a distinctive white patch on his chest.
He weighs 27 kilograms and stands 61 centimetres at the shoulder — larger than average for the breed.
His eyes are deep amber and his muzzle is unusually broad, giving him a distinctive expression.
He has a heavier, more powerful build than Csuszka, reflecting his stronger hunting lines.

Personality
-----------
Panir is bold, curious, and relentlessly enthusiastic. He approaches every situation as an opportunity for play.
He is highly intelligent but easily distracted, which made his training more challenging than Csuszka's.
He has a strong prey drive and will chase anything that moves, requiring a reliable recall before being let off-lead.
He is sociable with strangers and has never shown aggression, but he can be boisterous with smaller dogs.

Key life events
---------------
- September 2021: Arrived at his home in Budapest at 9 weeks old.
- January 2022: Enrolled in puppy obedience classes; passed with distinction.
- August 2022: Completed his first off-lead trail run of 8 kilometres in the Pilis mountains.
- March 2023: Placed second in the Junior class at the Hungarian Vizsla Speciality Show in Debrecen.
- October 2023: Passed his hunting field test (mezei vizsga) with a score of 87 out of 100.
- February 2024: Set his personal speed record of 48 km/h during a sprint exercise at the Csepel training ground.

Favourite activities
--------------------
Panir's greatest passion is running. He can sustain a fast pace for distances up to 12 kilometres before tiring.
His favourite terrain is open field, where he can range wide and use his nose freely.
He enjoys fetching tennis balls, but loses interest quickly if the game becomes repetitive.
He has a complicated relationship with water — he will enter shallow rivers enthusiastically but hesitates at deeper water.
His favourite treat is dried duck, which he will perform any behaviour to obtain.

Relationship with Csuszka
-------------------------
Panir idolises Csuszka and has modelled much of his behaviour on hers, including her love of water retrieval.
He defers to her in most situations despite being physically larger.
The two dogs engage in daily play sessions that last 20–30 minutes and typically end with both dogs exhausted and asleep.

Health
------
Panir has been healthy since birth with no significant medical history.
He was neutered in April 2023 following advice from his veterinarian.
He receives annual vaccinations and has been screened clear for hip dysplasia (HD-A rating) and elbow dysplasia.
He has a mild sensitivity to grain-based foods and is fed a grain-free kibble supplemented with raw meat twice weekly.
In December 2023 he strained his right front leg during a training session and required 3 weeks of rest.
